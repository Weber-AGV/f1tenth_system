              Vitals

                Unlock high speed?

                This raises the speed limit from 3 m/s to 5 m/s (about 18 km/h, 11 mph). At that speed:

                • The car covers 5 metres every second — across most rooms in two.
                • Your reaction time adds roughly 1.5 m before you even press STOP.
                • If Wi-Fi drops, the car keeps its last speed until the motor controller times out (about 1 s, not yet measured) — up to 5 m with nobody in control.
                • The car's motor controller (VESC) may cap speed lower than 5 m/s. This page cannot go past that limit.

                High speed switches off again when you leave the Drive tab or reload the page.

                Unlock full steering?

                This raises the steering limit from 19° (the normal controller limit) to 23°, the most the car allows.

                • A full turn at speed can tip the car over or spin it out. Slow down before turning hard.
                • Combined with high speed this is the easiest way to crash.
                • The servo can be pushed against its end stops, which wears it.
                • The motor controller (VESC) may limit steering further. This page cannot go past that limit.

                Full steering switches off again when you leave the Drive tab or reload the page.
